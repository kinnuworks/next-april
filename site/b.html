Executive summary

Every April to June, the oldest cabinets in VoltRelay’s EV battery-swap network overheat in Jaipur, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad and turn delivery riders away. A rule built from summer 2024 predicted summer 2025 for every station (R² 0.990 out of sample). Fund fixing those 36 cabinets, not more stations or more packs.

Key takeaways

The 36 Gen1 stations in Jaipur, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad handled 27.4% of summer 2025 attempts and produced 51.2% of its failures.

Questions

How the numbers were checked.

Where does the data come from?

The organisers' synthetic VoltRelay dataset: eight CSV files, every swap attempt from January 2024 to June 2025.

How were the data problems handled?

Every documented data issue checked, plus 6 the documentation does not mention, each with rows affected and its effect on a result.

Can I re-run it?

Every number is computed by a notebook that runs top to bottom from the organiser's files, and re-derived by independent tests.

Was anything tested out of sample?

Out-of-sample checks: the summer 2024 rule on summer 2025, the churn model on riders who started in 2025, a placebo test for the pricing pilot.
