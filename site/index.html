Operations briefing · VoltRelay battery-swap network

The same 36 old cabinets will fail again next April.

Every April to June, the oldest swap cabinets (Gen1) in Jaipur, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad overheat, charge slowly and turn riders away, even when packs show in stock. A rule built from summer 2024 alone predicted summer 2025 for every station open both summers. The 60 new stations VoltRelay opened in between were spread across all six cities, not placed where these failures happen.

Why it matters for riders

New riders' churn by the share of their swap attempts that failed in their first 14 days.

Why these numbers can be trusted

Every documented data issue checked, plus 6 the documentation does not mention, each with rows affected and its effect on a result.
Every number is computed by a notebook that runs top to bottom from the organiser's files, and re-derived by independent tests.
Out-of-sample checks: the summer 2024 rule on summer 2025, the churn model on riders who started in 2025, a placebo test for the pricing pilot.
Synthetic VoltRelay dataset provided by Gradient Learnings. AI coding assistance used; every number checked by the notebook, tests and a number-tracing script.
